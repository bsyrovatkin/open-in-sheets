The problem

Every .xlsx opens in the wrong app

Your export lands in Downloads. A double-click hands it to Numbers — but the work happens in Google Sheets. So you close it and move the file across by hand. Every single time.

No sharingNumbers gives you a file to email around — not a link, comments, and two people in the same sheet.
No ExcelWithout Office installed there is no second option. Dragging each file into Drive is the whole workflow.

Lives in the menu bar

Uploads and opens

Quit and it's undone

“Quit and restore Numbers” puts everything back.

One honest caveat

Drive gets a copy. Google Sheets cannot edit a local file, so edits in the Sheet do not flow back into the .xlsx on disk.
